Encounter Guide Highlights

🗺️

World Map Entrance Pins

Interactive Retail-style blue swirl indicators right on zone and world maps. Filter pins by minimum level or bracket, and click to jump straight to that dungeon's journal entry.

🎨

Official LFG Brackets & Difficulty

Level badges color-code in real time (Grey, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red) against your character level using authentic Blizzard relative difficulty algorithms.

🛡️

Capital City Faction Crests

Prominent, authentic Horde and Alliance crests on instance headers for capital city dungeons, including Ragefire Chasm, Ruins of Lordaeron, Hall of Thanes, and The Stockade.

⚔️

Tier 0.5 Dungeon Set Challenges

Dedicated encounter section for summonable Dungeon Set 2 (Tier 0.5) challenge bosses (Lord Valthalak, Mor Grayhoof, Jarien and Sothos, Kormok, Theldren, Isalien).

🔍

Real-Time Live Search

Instant responsive filtering across all 35 classic dungeons, bosses, rare spawns, aliases, and encounter loot, with synchronized cross-grid item discovery.

⚙️

Granular In-Game Settings

Full settings suite accessible via the minimap button or game options. Toggle level badges, difficulty colors, minor quest encounters, holiday schedules, and grid sorting.

Quick Installation

  1. Download the Addon Zip

    Click the download button above to grab Adventure_Journal-v0.4.0.zip.

  2. Extract to your WoW Forever AddOns Folder

    Unzip the archive directly into your installation directory:

    World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Adventure_Journal\

  3. Open the Journal In-Game

    Launch the game or type /reload if already logged in. Open your journal with /aj, /journal, or by clicking the gold minimap button.
