Adventure Journal

A modular in-game journal framework for World of Warcraft Forever. Currently providing comprehensive dungeon encounter and item lists, with planned modules for raids, professions, attunements, class quests, and more.

Feature Highlights

Architecture

Modular Architecture & Cross-Module Synergy

Engineered for deep cross-module integration—connecting map markers, encounter catalogs, global search, and future questlines into a unified reference tool—while maintaining a lightweight, intentional footprint that allows players to enable only the exact modules they need.

Roadmap

Planned Ecosystem Modules

Active development roadmap includes dedicated modules for Raid loot tables, Primary & Secondary Professions (recipes, reagents, and trainer sources), and Questlines (dungeon attunements and class quest chains).

Dungeon Module

Dungeon Item Lists & Encounters

Comprehensive directories across Classic Dungeons and WoW Forever Dungeons, with an extensible catalog built to seamlessly incorporate any future added instances, boss rosters, lore backgrounds, and encounter item drops.

Enhancements

Game Enhancements

Adventure-focused game enhancements with cross-module integration: native Blizzard map pin system integration for dungeon entrance portals, planned travel lane markers for zeppelins and boats, and more.

Search

Real-Time Live Search

Fast, responsive search filtering across dungeons, boss encounters, aliases, and specific item drops, with automatic cross-module encounter discovery.

Raid Module

Raid Encounters & Tier Sets

10, 20, and 40-player raid progression references covering Classic raids and confirmed WoW Forever endgame raids—engineered to adapt to future raid additions as they release, complete with boss mechanics, role notes, and tier set catalogs.

Development Context

Built with Agentic Coding

Adventure Journal was developed through agentic AI workflows to explore, test, and refine agent-driven software engineering workflows, skills, and practices using models from Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude).

Development Roadmap

Priority 1 • Next Up

Raid Journal & Attunements

10, 20, and 40-player raid progression references, boss mechanics, role notes, and tier set catalogs alongside attunements, targeting confirmed encounters: The Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, and Onyxia's Lair.

Priority 2 • Architecture

Quest Data Storage Overhaul & Refactor

Unified storage system with a scalable quest tagging system to match the item storage architecture, enabling rich cross-linking and multi-dimensional querying.

Priority 3 • Planned Tab

Standalone Quests Tab

Dedicated Quests tab for class quests, collectible item quests, and elite quests like attunements. Quests are already available in the dungeon tab, but this tab will allow for quest searching and act as a place to view priority quests.

Backlog

Future Explorations & Unscheduled Candidates

Candidate modules and feature ideas queued for future investigation without a fixed roadmap position:

  • Professions Module: Comprehensive recipe catalogs, crafting reagents, gathering nodes, and trainer reference directories.
  • Notes Module: In-game player notes, personal coordinate bookmarks, and custom adventure reminder checklists.
  • Compact Mode: Streamlined, low-profile interface option designed for active in-instance and quest tracking.
  • Gamepad & Controller Support: Investigation and potential implementation of native controller navigation and gamepad bindings.

Quick Installation

  1. Download the Addon Zip

    Click the download button above to grab Adventure_Journal-v0.7.2.zip.

  2. Extract to your WoW Forever AddOns Folder

    Unzip the archive directly into your installation directory:

    World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Adventure_Journal\

    Tip: You can easily locate your game folder by clicking the gear icon next to the Play button in the Battle.net launcher for your WoW Forever client and selecting "Show in Explorer".

    Important: It is strongly recommended when updating the Adventure_Journal that all old files and folders are completely removed before adding the newly downloaded ones.

  3. Open the Journal In-Game

    Launch the game or type /reload if already logged in. Open your journal with /aj, /journal, or by clicking the gold minimap button.
