Adventure Journal

A modular in-game journal framework for World of Warcraft Forever. Currently providing comprehensive dungeon encounter and item lists, with planned modules for raids, professions, attunements, class quests, and more.

Feature Highlights

Architecture

Modular Addon Framework

Built from the ground up as an extensible platform. Adventure Journal acts as a lightweight core shell hosting dedicated, plug-and-play modules for dungeon item lists, utilities, and future gameplay catalogs.

Roadmap

Planned Ecosystem Modules

Active development roadmap includes dedicated modules for Raid loot tables, Primary & Secondary Professions (recipes, reagents, and trainer sources), and Questlines (dungeon attunements and class quest chains).

Dungeon Module

Dungeon Item Lists & Encounters

Detailed directories across 35 Classic and custom WoW Forever dungeons, featuring full boss rosters, lore backgrounds, and encounter item drop catalogs.

Utility Module

World Map Entrance Indicators

Retail-style blue swirl portal markers directly on world and zone maps showing dungeon locations, featuring level-bracket visibility and one-click navigation directly into the journal.

Search

Real-Time Live Search

Fast, responsive search filtering across dungeons, boss encounters, aliases, and specific item drops, with automatic cross-grid encounter discovery.

Encounters

Tier 0.5 Challenge Bosses

Dedicated encounter section for summonable Dungeon Set 2 (Tier 0.5) challenge encounters, complete with elite medallion borders and challenge boss rosters.

Development Context

Built with Agentic Coding

Adventure Journal was developed through agentic AI workflows to explore, test, and refine agent-driven software engineering workflows, skills, and practices using Google models, including Flash 3.8, Hydrogen, and Argon.

Quick Installation

  1. Download the Addon Zip

    Click the download button above to grab Adventure_Journal-v0.4.0.zip.

  2. Extract to your WoW Forever AddOns Folder

    Unzip the archive directly into your installation directory:

    World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Adventure_Journal\

  3. Open the Journal In-Game

    Launch the game or type /reload if already logged in. Open your journal with /aj, /journal, or by clicking the gold minimap button.
